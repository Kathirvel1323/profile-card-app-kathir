# Profile Card enhancements

- Toggle the card surface between light and dark presentation.
- Copy email, phone, and location values with a confirmation message.
- Retains the required avatar, name, role, and contact rows in one responsive screen.

## Structure

- `lib/main.dart`: Material app, profile screen, and reusable contact row.
- `test/widget_test.dart`: verifies the required profile content.

## Demo checklist

1. Launch the app and confirm the profile content is visible.
2. Toggle the theme action in the app bar.
3. Tap a contact copy icon and confirm the copied feedback appears.
